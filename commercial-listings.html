Retail investing, in plain English

The concepts that drive every strip-mall deal — explained the way we'd explain them over coffee.

Cap rates

A cap rate is a property's annual net operating income divided by its price — a quick shorthand for yield. Higher isn't automatically better; it often reflects higher risk, older buildings, or weaker tenant mixes. We walk you through what the number actually means for a specific center.

NNN leases

In a triple-net (NNN) lease, the tenant pays taxes, insurance, and maintenance on top of rent. That can mean steadier hands-off income for the owner — but the fine print matters enormously. We read leases with you before you sign anything.

Tenant mix

A strip mall is only as strong as its tenants. National-credit anchors, staggered lease expirations, and internet-resistant uses (food, services, medical) are what separate durable income from a slow vacancy spiral.
