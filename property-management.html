Common Questions From Property Owners

Do you currently manage properties?

Not yet — we're launching, which is exactly why founding partners get our full attention and preferential pricing. You'll work directly with the founding team and help shape how we operate.

What size properties do you work with?

We focus on multi-family properties, from small duplexes up to large apartment complexes. We're especially interested in partnering with developers on new-build projects and investors with multi-unit portfolios. Single-family homes are not our focus.

Can you help with new construction lease-up?

Yes. We offer lease-up support including pre-construction planning, marketing strategy, tenant placement, and move-in coordination — and we can start working with you before construction is complete.

What makes VDI different from other property managers?

We're small on purpose right now: multi-family only, transparent written pricing, and you deal directly with the people running the business — not a call center queue.

How quickly can you take over management?

We'll map out a transition plan together after your consultation and put the timeline in writing before you sign anything. Typical handoffs run a few weeks, depending on the property.

Do you require long-term contracts?

You'll see the full agreement terms in your written proposal — including how either side can end it — before you commit to anything. No fine-print traps.

How will I see what's happening with my property?

Through clear monthly statements and direct access to the team managing your property. During your consultation we'll walk you through exactly how reporting and rent collection work.

How do I get started?

Schedule a free consultation below. We'll discuss your property, review your goals, and put together a written proposal. No obligation — just an honest conversation about whether we're a good fit.

How do you handle evictions?

We work to prevent them first — thorough screening, clear leases, and early intervention on late payments. When removal is unavoidable, we coordinate with qualified local eviction counsel and manage the process on your behalf, keeping you informed at every step.

Do I keep control of my property?

Yes. You set the goals and approve major decisions and spending above an agreed threshold. We run the day-to-day and keep you posted with regular statements.

I'm unhappy with my current manager. Can you take over?

We can help with that. We'll review your existing leases and financials, coordinate the handoff, notify tenants, and keep rent collection running during the switch.
